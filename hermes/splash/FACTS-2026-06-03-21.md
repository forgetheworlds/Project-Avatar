### F-2026-06-03-064 [HIGH] — DroneBridge for ESP32 Mavlink Bridge

Oscar Liang 2024 motor/prop table confirms 1505 3800KV on 4S with 3.5" triblade is near-spec for sub-250g freestyle. Recommended: motor 1404/1504/1604/2004, KV 3500-4000, battery 850-1000mAh, dry weight 150-200g.
