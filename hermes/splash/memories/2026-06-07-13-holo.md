### FACT-095: S18 Pump Weight Correction (CRITICAL)
**The S18 micro diaphragm pump weighs 35-40g (BLDC) or 40-45g (brushed), NOT 18g as previously stated.**
- Dimensions: BLDC 43×25×41mm, Brushed 55×25×41mm
- Flow rate: 260 mL/min (0.26 L/min)
- Max pressure: 3.5 bar (350 kPa)
- Power draw: 0.07-0.11A at 12V (negligible)
- Impact: Previous weight budgets were off by +17-22g for the pump alone
- Prior fact F-2026-06-01-042 needs correction

### FACT-096: Coreless Gear Pump (12-15g, 1-1.5m range)
**A DIY micro coreless gear pump at 12-15g is technically feasible but limited to ~1-1.5m range.**
- Components: 12mm coreless motor (7-10g) + PEEK/PPS gear housing (4-6g)
- Flow rate: 400-600 mL/min at 12V
- Pressure: ~1-2 bar (significantly less than S18)
- Weak self-priming — requires gravity-feed from elevated reservoir
- For v1 prototype, S18's 2-4m range is recommended over gear pump's 1-1.5m

### FACT-097: DroneBridge ESP32 v2.x Production-Ready
**DroneBridge for ESP32 v2.x is confirmed production-ready with ESP32-S3 native support.**
- Flashing: Chrome WebUSB tool (no toolchain needed)
- Wiring: ESP32 TX→FC RX, RX→FC TX, 5V→5V, GND→GND on UART4
- Configuration: SERIAL4_PROTOCOL=2, SERIAL4_BAUD=57 or 921
- Range: 150m WiFi AP, 1km+ ESP-NOW LR (with IPEX antenna)
- Latency: 2-8ms ESP-NOW, 10-20ms WiFi

**The S18 pump draws only 0.07-0.11A at 12V (<1.5W). Does not affect flight time calculations.**
