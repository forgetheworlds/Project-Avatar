## New Facts This Cycle

### F-2026-06-06-084 [CRITICAL] — Pump Weight Budget Crisis Confirmed
Pump weight confirmed as the #1 design constraint: 370 diaphragm pump = 60-65g (not 15-25g as originally budgeted), 385 pump = ~110g. Both exceed the original 50g total payload estimate. Only micro coreless gear pumps (12-15g, 400-600 mL/min, per F-2026-06-03-061) keep Splash under 250g — but their unknown pressure limits engagement range to ~1-1.5m.
**Action:** Test coreless gear pump pressure vs range. If under 1m, accept >250g AUW or use 370 pump at ~90g total payload with tube frame.

### F-2026-06-06-085 [HIGH] — MG90S Definitive Servo Choice
MG90S (13g, metal gears, ball bearing, 2.2 kg·cm at 6V) definitively beats SG90 (9g, plastic gears, bushing, 1.8 kg·cm at 4.8V) for Splash pan-tilt. SG90 WILL strip plastic gears under recoil and continuous pan. Use MG90S on BOTH axes at 6V via separate BEC. The 8g total penalty vs SG90 is negligible compared to reliability.
**Action:** Order 2× MG90S servos. Add 5V/6V selectable BEC (~3g) to BOM.

### F-2026-06-06-086 [HIGH] — MOSFET Correction: Must Use Logic-Level
IRFZ44N requires Vgs ≥ 10V for full saturation — FC 3.3V/5V GPIO forces linear/active region → overheating. Must use logic-level MOSFET: IRL3705N or IRLZ44N (saturates at 3.3-5V). Required circuit: 220Ω gate resistor, 10kΩ pull-down, 1N4007 flyback diode across pump terminals, and common ground for all systems.
**Action:** Update wiring diagram. Replace IRFZ44N with IRL3705N/IRLZ44N in BOM.

### F-2026-06-06-087 [HIGH] — Flexible IV Bag Optimal Reservoir
Flexible IV bag/TPU bladder (1-2g dry weight) is the optimal reservoir. Beats rigid tank (4-6g with slosh) and syringe (6-9g with CG shift). Zero free-surface effect — bag collapses as water draws, NO sloshing, perfect CG. 15mL yields ~3-9 short bursts or ~3-5 protection-mode engagements.
**Action:** Source medical IV bags or 3D-print TPU bladders. Add magnetic quick-release + Luer-lock to mount design.

### F-2026-06-06-088 [MEDIUM] — Splash Occupies Novel Market Gap
No existing sub-250g drone water gun (projectile) found. Three competitor classes: Micro Wi-Fi Squirter (35-50g, 0.5-1m — dropper, not gun), Mid-Tier FPV Stracker (650-950g, 2-4m — closest but 2.5× Splash weight), Heavy-Lift Custom Lifter (1.8-2.8kg, 5-10m). Twelve documented failure modes from prior builds — all already have mitigations in Splash FMEA (F-2026-06-05-072).
**Action:** Document Splash as first-in-class sub-250g water projectile drone.
