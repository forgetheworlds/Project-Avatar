## FACT-038: LAB COLOR SPACE SUPERIOR TO HSV FOR TEAM JERSEY IDENTIFICATION
**Severity: HIGH | Category: CV/Tracking**
LAB (CIELAB) color space is better than HSV for outdoor team jersey identification because L* (lightness) is completely decoupled from a*/b* (color). HSV's H channel becomes unstable when lighting drops. Implementation: CLAHE pre-processing → BGR→LAB conversion → Otsu thresholding on a*/b* channels + static LAB range calibration → morphological cleanup. For Splash: run YOLO person detection first, crop bbox, apply LAB color filter (~2ms overhead). Red jersey: a* > 145, b* < 140. Blue jersey: a* < 130, b* < 115.

## FACT-039: BYTETRACK SELECTED AS SPLASH MULTI-PERSON TRACKING ALGORITHM
**Severity: MEDIUM | Category: CV/Tracking**
ByteTrack selected over DeepSORT for Splash. ByteTrack: Kalman + high/low confidence association, medium computational overhead, handles occlusion by retaining low-score boxes via IoU matching. DeepSORT Re-ID generates heavy CNN overhead. Native YOLO integration: `results = model.track(frame, persist=True, tracker="bytetrack.yaml", classes=[0])`. Lucas-Kanade optical flow and standard Kalman filter are insufficient for drone multi-person tracking due to fast ego-motion. Motion compensation via IMU gyro homography (existing FACT-002) is mandatory to prevent turret oscillation.
**Action:** Configure YOLO pipeline with ByteTrack tracker. Implement motion compensation wrapper class.

## FACT-040: NOZZLE DESIGN — 3.0-4.5MM ORIFICE, 12-15° CONE, PETG VERTICAL PRINT
**Severity: HIGH | Category: Water Gun Hardware**
Optimal nozzle for Splash: 3.0-4.5mm orifice diameter (smaller creates backpressure for diaphragm pump, larger increases water weight), 12-15° included contraction cone angle (gentle taper prevents boundary layer separation), laminar flow honeycomb matrix (15-20mm length before cone), straight discharge tip (length = 2× orifice). Real-world range: 2-4m with micro-diaphragm pump at 4-12 PSI (sufficient for 3-5m Senior Assassins engagements).
