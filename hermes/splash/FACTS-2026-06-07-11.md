### 1. Conformal Coating Procedure (MG Chemicals 422B)
**Severity:** HIGH | **Category:** Integration
- 2 thin brush coats, 5-8 min tack-free between sides, 48h full room-temp cure
- 20 min at 65°C for accelerated cure
- UV tracer fluoresces blue — verify coverage with blacklight
- **DO coat:** All SMD, MCU legs, solder pads, capacitor legs
- **DO NOT coat:** Barometer hole, USB/SD ports, buttons, JST pins, motor stators
- **Solder-through:** 422B vaporizes under soldering iron — re-coat after repair

### 5. Pump Bench Test — 1.8mm Nozzle Sweet Spot
**Severity:** HIGH | **Category:** Water Gun
- Bodenflo/Hilintec S18 18g coreless diaphragm pump: 0.60 L/min free flow, 2.5 bar max
- 1.8mm nozzle: 1.4 bar backpressure, 0.32 L/min, 0.42-0.58m range bench
- Prop wash halves effective range (~0.3m airborne vs ~0.5m bench)
- Recoil force: ~0.011 N (1.13g) — negligible flight impact
- Larger pumps needed for >2m range (R385 60g or accumulator impulse system)
