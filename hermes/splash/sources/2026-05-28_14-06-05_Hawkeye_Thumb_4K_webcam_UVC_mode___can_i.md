**No**, the

Hawkeye Thumb 4K cannot simultaneously stream a 1080p UVC webcam feed over USB and record a 4K file to its internal microSD card. `[19][20][21][22][23][24]`

When you connect the camera to a computer via USB-C, you must choose a distinct, single operating state. `[13][14][15][16][17][18]`

System Limitations 

* **UVC Protocol Lock**: Activating **PC CAM (Webcam) Mode** locks the processing chip into a streaming state. The single physical button changes functionality or disables the local onboard digital video recorder (DVR) entirely.
* **Storage Mode Conflict**: Connecting the USB-C cable otherwise forces the camera into **U-Disk (Mass Storage) Mode** to transfer files. It treats the internal microSD card like a card reader, making it inaccessible for active onboard recording.
* **Processor Bandwidth Limits**: The lightweight processing architecture used in ultra-micro FPV thumb cameras cannot handle the parallel video encoding pipelines needed to output an uncompressed/MJPEG live USB stream while writing an H.265 4K 30/60fps stream to an SD card. `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`

Dual-Role FPV Setup Alternatives 

If you need a dual-role setup (simultaneous live pilot viewing and high-res recording), you can utilize the camera's hardware splitting features: 

* **Simultaneous TV-Out and Onboard 4K Recording**: The camera features a dedicated 4-pin hardware port on the back. This port outputs a low-latency (approx. 40–50ms) analog video signal (PAL/NTSC) to FPV goggles or an analog video transmitter while simultaneously recording crystal-clear 4K video directly to the microSD card.
* **Converting to a Stream**: To turn this dual-role setup into a live PC stream, you can route the camera's analog TV-out signal into an **analog-to-USB UVC video capture card** (like a Rotguts receiver or an EasyCap dongle) connected to your computer. `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`
