**Senior Assassins** is a high-stakes, real-world elimination game where participants track down and "eliminate" assigned targets. While traditionally a high school graduation tradition, the game has evolved significantly when adapted for university and college campuses. 

This breakdown covers the standard 2026 rules, gameplay mechanics, and structural differences tailored for a sprawling university environment. 

---

📅 The University Timeline 

Because university schedules differ from high schools, campus games are usually compressed into a high-intensity, month-long event during the spring semester. 

*

* **Week 1 (Registration & Setup):** Players pay an entry fee (usually $5 to $10) to build the cash prize pool. Targets are randomly and secretly assigned via tracking apps like Splashin. 

* **Weeks 2–4 (Elimination Rounds):** Players have strict 7-day windows to eliminate their assigned target. If you fail to eliminate your target by the end of the round, you are automatically disqualified. 

* **The Climax (The Purge & Finale):** In the final week, organizers call a "Purge." All safety items are disabled. The game switches to a "last person standing" free-for-all until a grand prize winner is crowned. 

*

🛡️ University Gameplay Mechanics 

1. Target Progression & Verification 

*

* **The Chain:** When you successfully eliminate your target, you inherit *their* target automatically. 

* **Video Proof:** Every single elimination must be caught on clear video and sent to the game administrators. If it is not on camera, the kill does not count. 

* **Tracking:** Many university games require players to join a shared [Life360](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXLErRHlahW/) circle or use specialized apps to monitor their targets' movements across campus. 

*

2. Campus Safe Zones 

Unlike high school games, which take place entirely off-campus, university games happen on campus but heavily restrict where the "assassinations" can legally occur. 

*

* **Strict Off-Limits Areas:** Inside classrooms, university libraries, lecture halls, campus laboratories, dining halls, and places of worship.

* **The Dorm Rule:** Inside a target's assigned residence hall room or apartment is a safe zone unless you are explicitly invited inside by the resident. Courtyards and communal dorm hallways are generally fair game.

* **Workplace Exemptions:** If a student works an on-campus job (e.g., at the campus bookstore or gym), they cannot be targeted while on the clock. 

*

3. Immunity and "The Uniform" 

To survive while walking across open campus quads, players must utilize immunity mechanics: 

*

* **The Goggles Rule:** Wearing **swim goggles** visible on your forehead or neck grants total immunity from being shot.

* **The Variant Days:** Administrators may announce "Underwater Days" (immunity only counts if goggles are worn completely over your eyes) or "Purge Days" (goggles provide zero protection). 

*

4. Vehicle Safety Rules 

Due to severe real-world accidents, modern rules carry a **zero-tolerance policy** for vehicles: 

*

* No shooting out of moving cars, no drive-bys, and no car chases.

* Targets can only be shot if the vehicle is fully parked, and windows or doors are manually opened.
