For a drone-mounted water gun system in 2026, **micro diaphragm pumps are significantly better than peristaltic pumps** because they deliver the high pressure and flow rates required to shoot a stream of water, while maintaining an excellent power-to-weight ratio. `[13][14][15][16]`

Peristaltic pumps excel at precision dosing and handling sensitive fluids, but they have low maximum output pressures, very low flow rates, and heavy gearboxes that limit drone flight time and water projectile distance. 

---

Core Technology Comparison 

Diaphragm Pumps (Recommended) 

* **Mechanism**: A reciprocating flexible diaphragm creates alternating pressure chambers using one-way check valves.
* **Drone Advantage**: They are lightweight, highly efficient, deliver up to 130 PSI of projectile pressure, and support high flow rates.
* **Trade-off**: The stream experiences brief pressure pulses, which can be easily smoothed out with a tiny inline expansion chamber or a nozzle reservoir. 

Peristaltic Pumps 

* **Mechanism**: Rotating rollers compress a flexible tube to push fluid forward.
* **Drone Disadvantage**: To achieve even a moderate flow rate, they require heavy high-torque motors and thick casings.
* **Pressure Limit**: They rarely exceed 15–25 PSI, which results in a weak, drooping water stream rather than a shooting projectile.
