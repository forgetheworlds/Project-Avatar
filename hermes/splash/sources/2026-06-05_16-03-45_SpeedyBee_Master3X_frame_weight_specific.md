The **[SpeedyBee Master3X Frame](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-master3x-frame/)** is a modular 3 to 3.6-inch drone frame built primarily for digital freestyle platforms like the DJI O3 and O4 Pro Air Units. `[13][14][15][16][17][18]`

While it delivers incredible structural protection, its heavy frame weight makes achieving a **sub-250g build extremely difficult**—though still possible with carefully curated ultra-light components. 

---

📊 Frame Specifications & Weight 

*

* **Wheelbase:** 171mm

* **Base Frame Weight:** 87g ± 2g (bare frame without the modular camera head)

* **Total Frame Weight:** ~145g (fully assembled with the O4P Aluminum Alloy Head and TPU accessories)

* **Carbon Thickness:** 4.0mm arms; 2.0mm top, middle, and bottom plates

* **Stack Mounting:** 25.5 × 25.5mm (AIO style)

* **Stack Height Space:** 11mm (very tight clearance)

* **Motor Mounting:** 9mm to 12mm M2 pattern

* **Camera Pitch:** 19mm / 20mm micro cameras 

*

---

🛡️ Durability Assessment 

*

* **True Freestyle Toughness:** With 4mm thick carbon fiber arms, the core frame easily survives hard concrete or asphalt impacts. Pilot reviews state that after multiple crashes, the carbon body remains structurally unblemished. 

* **CNC Aluminum Nose Protection:** The modular head section uses rigid CNC aluminum side plates and reinforced standoffs. This isolates your expensive DJI O3 or O4 Pro Air Unit from front-end impacts. 

* **Integrated Prop Clearance:** The frame features a unique "balanced head lock" that shifts the FPV camera forward. This layout keeps your propellers completely out of the FPV view and prevents prop strikes against your camera casing during crashes. `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`

*

---

🛠️ Build Log & Anatomy 

Building on the [Master3X frame](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-master3x-drone/) requires strict component placement due to space constraints: 

```
      [ CNC Aluminum Nose ]  --> Houses & cools DJI O4/O3 Air Unit
               ||
     [ 21mm Low-Profile Body ] --> Holds 25.5mm AIO Board (11mm stack limit)
               ||
   [ XT30 & Niche Balance Bay ] --> Captures power leads away from props
               ||
       [ Integrated GPS ]    --> Tail mount fits an 18.1x18.1mm unit

```

1. **The Modular Head:** The digital VTX and camera drop directly into the front aluminum module. This pod can be swapped out onto other compatible frames (like the Bee25) via 4 screws without tearing down the rest of your quad. 
2. **Tight Stack Clearance:** The 11mm deck height means you must use an **all-in-one (AIO) flight controller/ESC board**. There is virtually no room to stack a separate 4-in-1 ESC and FC. 
3. **Capacitor & Cable Management:** Solder your power capacitor flat against the frame or out the back. The frame features custom molded wire guards, a built-in beeper, and a hard-mounted XT30 connector slot to keep cables from shifting. 

---

⚖️ The Sub-250g Dilemma & Component Strategy 

A standard manufacturer build utilizing 1507 motors and an 850mAh 4S battery pushes the total flying weight to roughly **335g**. Because the frame kit alone weighs roughly 145g with its metal head, you have a strict **105g budget** for motors, electronics, props, and a battery to stay sub-250g. 

To beat the regulatory weight ceiling, you must intentionally under-spec the build: 

| Component | Sub-250g Target Choice | Estimated Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Motors** | 1404 or 1503 lightweight brushless (Avoid heavy 1507 or 2004 motors) | ~36g (9g each) |
| **AIO Board** | Lightweight 25.5mm F405 / 35A AIO | ~8g |
| **VTX/Camera** | DJI O3 Air Unit (naked/decased) or Walksnail Moonlight | ~28g-35g |
| **Props** | Thin, light 3-inch or 3.5-inch bi-blades | ~4g |
