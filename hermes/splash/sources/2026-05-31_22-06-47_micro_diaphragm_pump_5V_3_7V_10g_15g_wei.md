Analysis of Micro Components (3.7V / 5V) 

* **The 10g - 15g Weight Class (030 Series):** These miniature pumps utilize an 030-size motor. When restricted to a 3.7V lithium battery or a 5V drone logic board power supply, they achieve an optimal low-volume spray output of roughly **48ml to 150ml per minute** depending on the backpressure and nozzle restriction. 
* **Self-Priming Ability:** Micro diaphragm mechanisms are naturally self-priming. They can purge air from a miniature payload line autonomously without requiring a manual gravity feed. `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`
* **Drone Integration:** Due to drawing low current, these micro pumps can be powered directly from a 5V BEC (Voltage Regulator) on a flight controller or straight from a single-cell (1S) 3.7V LiPo battery. 
*

Industrial Drone Reality (Hobbywing & Foxtech) 

If you are trying to match these specifications to official components from Hobbywing or Foxtech systems: 

* **Hobbywing XRotor Pumps:** Their smallest signature system is the[Hobbywing XRotor 5L Pump](https://www.hobbywingdirect.com/products/eps-pump-5l), weighing 388g with a 5,000ml/min flow rate.
* **Voltage Mismatch:** They do not design 3.7V or 5V water pumps because professional agricultural spraying platforms require massive pressure to atomize liquid through pressure nozzles across multiple meters of boom arms. 
*

To build a payload in the 10g–15g footprint, look for **030 coreless micro-diaphragm liquid pumps** or **130-motor micro pumps** rather than heavy-duty commercial drone catalogs.
