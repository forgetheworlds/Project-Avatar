Applying agricultural drone technology to an FPV water gun drone allows you to maximize flight time and spray efficiency. Scaled-down agricultural engineering principles offer direct solutions for miniature payloads. `[7][8][9]`

Pump Selection: Centrifugal vs. Diaphragm `[4][5][6]`

* **Centrifugal Pumps**: Best for high-volume, continuous streams. They are lightweight and offer smooth flow but lack self-priming capabilities and fail if air enters the line.
* **Diaphragm Pumps**: Best for precise, high-pressure bursts. They are self-priming, can run dry without damage, and maintain steady pressure, making them ideal for combat/shooting FPV drones. 

Component Design & Weight Optimization 

* **Reservoir Tank**: Use 3D-printed TPU or thin-walled PETG with internal baffles to stop water sloshing from disrupting FPV flight stability.
* **Nozzle Dynamics**: Select solid-stream miniature nozzles to maintain a tight water beam over a distance, preventing the atomization (misting) favored by farmers.
* **Weight Control**: Keep the fluid payload under 25% of the drone's total takeoff weight to preserve agile FPV handling characteristics. `[1][2][3]`

Flow Control & Electronics 

* **PWM Control**: Connect the pump motor speed controller (ESC) to a spare PWM channel on your flight controller to adjust water pressure via your radio transmitter.
* **Dynamic Mixing**: Set up an OpenTX/EdgeTX radio mix that links the pump trigger to your throttle joystick to automatically compensate for altitude changes. 

Key Agricultural Lessons for FPV Drones 

* **Center of Gravity**: Mount the water tank directly under the center of the frame so the drone stays balanced as the fluid drains.
* **Pressure Management**: Install a tiny micro-bypass valve to protect your miniature pump lines from bursting when the nozzle is abruptly closed.
