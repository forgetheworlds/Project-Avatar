* **Flight Controller Capability:** The STM32H743 processor on the
  MicoAir AIO is incredibly powerful. While a bit premium for a 3.5-inch micro drone, its massive memory allows you to easily flash complex **Ardupilot** or INAV logic alongside the AM32 ESC firmware, allowing for advanced waypoint navigation or autonomous rescue paths utilizing the Flywoo GOKU M10 GPS tracker. 

* **Video Layout Warning:** The Hawkeye Thumb series outputs an ultra-low latency analog signal (0.04s) for standard FPV piloting while internally recording stabilized 4K footage. Ensure you carefully map your power leads, as these modules are known to use inverted pinouts that can trigger an immediate short-circuit if plugged directly into standard layout boards without re-pinning.
