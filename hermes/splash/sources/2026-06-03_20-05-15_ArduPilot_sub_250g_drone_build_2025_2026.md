Building a sub-250g autonomous drone running [ArduPilot](https://ardupilot.org/) requires highly specialized workflows compared to traditional 5-inch or 10-inch builds. Because sub-250g micro-drones have incredibly high motor RPMs and low physical inertia, they are highly sensitive to motor noise, requiring strict filtering and rapid control loop adjustments. 

---

* **Flight Controller**: Use an AIO (All-In-One) board with an H7 or high-memory F7 processor (e.g., *Matek H743-AIO* or *[DarwinFPV High-Current AIO Go to product viewer dialog for this item.](/search?ibp=oshop&prds=pvt:hg,pvo:29,imageDocid:8252828023978597746,gpcid:5347447047851480950,headlineOfferDocid:1279343870366538635,catalogid:3043654102009800870,productDocid:9803260373496222771,rds:PC_5347447047851480950%7CPROD_PC_5347447047851480950&q=product&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwiK0_vcpOyUAxU2kIkEHclwNwEQxa4PeggIAggACBEQAw)*). * **GPS & Compass**: Choose micro modules like the *[Foxeer M10 Mini Go to product viewer dialog for this item.](/search?ibp=oshop&prds=pvt:hg,pvo:29,imageDocid:8300995738484999629,headlineOfferDocid:12813058267194247120,productDocid:12813058267194247120,rds:PC_13601481948555465137%7CPROD_PC_13601481948555465137&q=product&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwiK0_vcpOyUAxU2kIkEHclwNwEQxa4PeggIAggACBEQCA)* or *[Flywoo GOKU GM10 Nano V3 Go to product viewer dialog for this item.](/search?ibp=oshop&prds=pvt:hg,pvo:29,imageDocid:6959712396546693578,gpcid:4824037320009336272,headlineOfferDocid:16902706186477679723,catalogid:1630642900458454230,productDocid:9620140613872449555,rds:PC_4824037320009336272%7CPROD_PC_4824037320009336272&q=product&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwiK0_vcpOyUAxU2kIkEHclwNwEQxa4PeggIAggACBEQCg)*. 2. Parameter Tuning Setup (The Initial Micro-Tune) 

Do **NOT** attempt to fly a sub-250g drone on default ArduPilot parameters; its high-frequency physics will trigger a dramatic flyaway or instant motor burnout due to excessive PID feedback loop over-corrections. 

Before your first flight, open **Mission Planner**, navigate to the **Config/Tuning** tab, press **Alt + A** on your keyboard to open the **Initial Tuning Calculator Setup Wizard**, and input your specific sub-250g physical variables: 

* Select a **3-inch propeller size**.
* Input your specific battery cell count and chemistry (e.g., 4S LiPo).
* Enable **DShot600** or **DShot300** digital ESC communication, synchronizing the scheduled loop rate to **800Hz**. 

This calculator will dynamically rewrite your baseline parameters. It drops the default large-copter PID gains down to safe micro-quad values:  ini

```
# Core Roll/Pitch Angular Rate Constraints & Baseline PID Scalings
ATC_ACCEL_P_MAX   = 110000  # Sets quick, sharp micro-copter target angular pitch acceleration
ATC_ACCEL_R_MAX   = 110000  # Sets quick, sharp micro-copter target angular roll acceleration
ATC_ANG_PIT_P     = 6.0     # Lowers aggressive angular attitude correction loop values
ATC_ANG_RLL_P     = 6.0     # Lowers aggressive angular attitude correction loop values
INS_GYRO_FILTER   = 60      # Raises gyro low-pass cutoff filter frequency for fast-spinning micro motors

```

Use code with caution.

Harmonic Notch Filtering (Mandatory) 

Small drones exhibit high-frequency motor vibrations. You **must** utilize the ArduPilot Harmonic Notch Filter linked to your ESC telemetry data: 

1. Connect your ESC telemetry wire to an open Flight Controller UART RX pin, or utilize bidirectional DShot.
2. Set `INS_HNOTCH_ENABLE = 1`.
3. Set `INS_HNOTCH_MODE = 3` (ESC Telemetry RPM Tracking).
4. Set `INS_HNOTCH_HMNCS = 7` (Enables the fundamental notch, plus 1st and 2nd harmonics to isolate propwash).

4. Safety Configuration & Geofencing 

A flyaway sub-250g drone can easily be carried off by high winds due to its low weight. Strict geofencing and failsafe barriers are critical. 

Geofence Parameters 

Set up a localized proactive fence via the **Config -> GeoFence** menu in Mission Planner: 

* `FENCE_ACTION = 1` (Force the vehicle to immediately execute an automated Return-To-Launch **RTL** when crossing fence boundaries).
* `FENCE_TYPE = 7` (Enables a 3D bubble combining Max Altitude, Circle Radius, and Polygon boundaries).
* `FENCE_ALT_MAX = 120` (Sets a maximum vertical cap of 120 metres to strictly comply with international sub-250g open-category regulatory ceilings).
* `FENCE_RADIUS = 300` (Restricts horizontal radius to 300 metres from home position). 

Failsafe Variables 

* `FS_THR_ENABLE = 1` (Enables automated RTL upon critical Radio Control transmission packet loss or signal disconnect).
* `FS_BATT_VOLTAGE = 14.0` (Triggers low battery warning / automated RTL at a specific voltage floor, calculated as per cell for a 4S LiPo battery pack).
* `FS_GCS_ENABLE = 1` (Forces RTL if the telemetry data stream link to your ground station control app drops).

5. Autonomous Orbit (Circle Mode) & Mission Command 

ArduPilot handles circular trajectories natively via **Circle Mode** or programmatic **Waypoint Missions**. 

Method A: Guided Mode Live Command 

1. Take off and switch the drone into **Loiter Mode**.
2. Right-click the interactive map screen in **Mission Planner**.
3. Select **Fly To Here -> Circle** to dynamically trigger an orbit. 

Method B: Structured Autonomous Waypoint Mission 

To permanently program an absolute geometric orbit payload scanning profile, open the **Plan** screen and add structural navigation command items: 

1. `LOITER_TURNS`: Specify the coordinate center point, target speed, and exact number of full revolutions.
2. `ORBIT`: Specify the physical center coordinates, circle radius (meters), velocity, and camera gimbal pitch control parameters.  
Ensure you adjust `WPNAV_SPEED` downward (e.g., `300` cm/s or

6. PID Tuning for a Small Payload `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`

Adding a small payload (e.g., a micro action camera like a RunCam Thumb, Naked GoPro, or thermal sensor) alters the center of gravity and increases rotational inertia. 

Step-by-Step Tuning Workflow 

1. **Balance the Frame**: Ensure the payload is physically positioned directly over the drone's true center of gravity. Balance errors force motors to work asymmetric loads, generating yaw drift. `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`
2. **Apply QuikTune**: ArduPilot 4.6+ incorporates **QWIKTUNE**. Assign an RC channel switch to activate this script. Bring the drone to a stable hover in **Loiter Mode** outdoors on a calm day, then flip the switch. The flight controller will deliberately induce automated mini-axis oscillations, calculate the real-world physical responses of the added payload, and rewrite stable baseline PID values live. 
3. **Manual Fine-Tuning**: If the craft feels loose or displays "wobbling" or slow position drift after carrying the payload, marginally scale your position and rate parameters:
  * Gradually increase `ATC_RAT_RLL_P` and `ATC_RAT_PIT_P` in small steps to stiffen stick tracking and eliminate sluggish errors.
  * If high-pitched motor "whining" or physical high-frequency oscillations occur, scale down the derivative term `ATC_RAT_RLL_D` and `ATC_RAT_PIT_D` to stop the control loop from over-reacting to motor noise.
