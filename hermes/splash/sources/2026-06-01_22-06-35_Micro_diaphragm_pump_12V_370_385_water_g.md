Drone Payload Integration Strategies 

* **Mounting**: Use a [silicone shockproof sleeve](https://www.amazon.ca/Diaphragm-Electric-Water-Discharge-Accessory/dp/B0DD41RNNX) to eliminate high-frequency motor vibrations. Vibrations compromise the drone’s inertial measurement unit (IMU) and flight stabilization. `[11][12][13][14][15]`
* **Power & Control**: Do not power the pump directly from the flight controller. Use a separate Electronic Speed Controller (ESC) or an electronic relay switch connected to the drone's LiPo battery balance lead. `[6][7][8][9][10]`
* **Fluid Dynamics**: Place the liquid storage tank directly over the drone's center of gravity (CoG). Use slosh baffles inside the tank to prevent liquid movement from destabilizing autonomous routing. `[1][2][3][4][5]`
