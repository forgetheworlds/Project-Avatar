Key Takeaways from Reviews (e.g., Oscar Liang) 

* **Ultra-Lightweight Design:** Weighing a mere **15.5 grams**, it adds virtually no bulk, making it a perfect fit for sub-250g micro quads and cinewhoops. 
* **No Internal Battery:** To keep the weight down, the camera lacks an internal power source. It must be powered directly from your drone's flight controller or flight battery balance lead. 
* **Gyroflow Compatibility:** The camera writes standalone `.GCSV` gyro files alongside the video tracks. These sync seamlessly into [Gyroflow](https://gyroflow.xyz/) for silky smooth cinematic output, though users note the lens profile requires proper tuning to eliminate black crop borders. 
* **Video Quality Nuances:** While it captures up to 4K resolution, reviewers point out its relatively low **30–35Mbps bitrate** using the H.265 codec. The image can appear overly contrasty or lacking fine detail compared to heavy, full-sized action cameras. 
* **Integrated Mount:** The default plastic housing features a built-in 3mm GoPro-style dual-prong mount, eliminating the need for custom 3D prints.
