To meet your strict drone payload restriction of **sub-20 grams** while running on a **12V power supply**, standard hobbyist diaphragm pumps (like the common R385, which weighs ~110g) are far too heavy. 

To achieve your target weight, you must utilize industrial-grade **miniature coreless motor diaphragm pumps** or advanced **piezoelectric vibration pumps**. 

The lightest micro water pump options meeting these exact criteria include: 

1. Bodenflo / Hilintec S18 Series (Lightest Coreless Diaphragm) 

Industrial micro-pump manufacturers like [Bodenflo](https://bodenpump.com/micro-diaphragm-pump/) and [Hilintec](https://mini-pump.com/product/s18-high-cost-performance-mini-liquid-pump/) produce specialized space-constrained variants. 

* **Weight**: **18 grams**
* **Mechanism**: Rotary coreless DC motor driving a micro-diaphragm.
* **Voltage**: 12V DC variant available.
* **Specs**: Delivers roughly 350 mL/min to 800 mL/min of liquid flow.
* **Drone Suitability**: This is the absolute lightest mechanical, self-priming diaphragm pump available. It provides enough pressure for micro-spraying or liquid sampling payloads. 

2.

Takasago SDMP / APP Piezoelectric Series

(Lightest Vibration) `[5][6][7][8]`

If your payload requires precision, minimal electrical noise, and the absolute lowest profile, a piezoelectric vibration pump is your best alternative. 

* **Weight**: **~10 grams** 
* **Mechanism**: Piezoelectric bimorph vibration disk acting as a diaphragm. 

* **Voltage**: The pump element itself runs via high-frequency AC pulses, but it is typically paired with a tiny **12V DC or 5V DC micro-driver board** (such as the
  Sandur MPD-200A or
  Aurora Pro driver
, which adds just 2.7g to 9g of weight). 
* **Specs**: ~20 mL/min to 30 mL/min flow rate. 
* **Drone Suitability**: Ideal if your water-pumping goal is high-accuracy micro-dosing or lab-on-a-drone environmental sampling. However, the flow rate is much lower than motor-driven pumps.

Important Drone Integration Tips 

* **Vibration Dampening**: Because both options are vibration or reciprocating diaphragm-based, use a tiny strip of silicone or foam tape to prevent the pump's pulses from interfering with your drone's flight controller IMU / gyroscopes. 
* **Tubing Weight**: At sub-20 grams, the weight of the water inside the tubes often exceeds the pump weight. Use ultra-thin **2mm or 3mm ID polyurethane/silicone tubing** to minimize the fluid weight footprint during flight.
