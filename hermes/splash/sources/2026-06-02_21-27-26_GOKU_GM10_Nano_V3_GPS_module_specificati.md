Technical Specifications 

* **Chipset**: u-blox M10050 (10th Generation Chip)
* **Dimensions**: 12mm × 16mm × 4.5mm (Standard) or 12mm × 17mm × 5mm (Compass variant)
* **Frequency Bands**: GPS L1, GLONASS L1, BeiDou B1, Galileo E1, SBAS L1, QZSS
* **Channels**: 72 Searching Channels
* **Baud Rate**: 4800bps to 921600bps (Default: 115200bps)
* **Update Rate**: 1Hz–10Hz (Default: 10Hz)
* **Input Voltage**: 3.3V–5V DC
* **Protocol Compatibility**: `UBLOX` (Betaflight 4.3.x+) or `NMEA` (Betaflight 4.2.x and earlier) 

Weight 

* **Standard Version**: **2.2g** to **2.5g**.
* **Compass Version**: **2.6g** (includes an integrated QMC5883L magnetometer/compass). 

Lock Time & Sensitivity 

* **Cold Start Lock Time**: **~60 seconds** under an open sky.
* **Warm Start Lock Time**: **1 to 2 seconds** (nearly instantaneous due to onboard data retention).
* **Tracking Sensitivity**: **-162dBm**.
* **Reacquisition Sensitivity**: **-160dBm**. 

Accuracy 

The module features a high positional accuracy within **1.5 to 2.0 metres** under ideal outdoor environments. This precision stems from its capability to concurrent-track up to **four global satellite constellations simultaneously** (GPS + Galileo + BeiDou + GLONASS), vastly lowering its position dilution of precision (PDOP).
