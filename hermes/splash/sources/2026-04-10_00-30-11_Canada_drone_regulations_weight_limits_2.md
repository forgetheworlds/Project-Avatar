In Canada, drone regulations distinguish between **microdrones** (under 250g) and **small drones** (250g to 25kg). Starting in late 2025 and continuing into 2026, Transport Canada has updated these rules to include new categories like "medium drones" and expanded privileges for advanced pilots. `[13][14][15]` **Microdrones (Under 250g)** 

Drones weighing less than 250g, such as the DJI Mini series, are subject to fewer requirements but must still be operated safely. `[10][11][12]`

* **No Registration**: You do not need to register the drone with [Transport Canada](https://tc.canada.ca/en).
* **No Pilot Certificate**: A drone pilot certificate is not required for standard recreational flight.
* **Safety Rule (CAR 900.06)**: You must never fly in a "reckless or negligent manner" that endangers aviation safety or people.
* **Restricted Areas**: You are prohibited from entering restricted airspace (Class F), emergency sites (e.g., forest fires), and **National Parks** without a permit.
* **Advertised Events**: Under 2025/2026 rules, flying a microdrone at an **advertised event** (e.g., outdoor concerts, festivals) now requires a **Special Flight Operations Certificate (SFOC)**.  **Small Drones (250g to 25kg) - Basic Operations** `[7][8][9]`

If your drone weighs 250g or more, you must follow the rules for "small drones". **Basic operations** are flights in uncontrolled airspace, at least 30m away from bystanders. 

* **Registration**: You must register your drone through the [Drone Management Portal](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety/registering-your-drone) ($5 fee) and mark it visibly with its registration number.
* **Pilot Certificate**: You must be at least **14 years old** and pass the **Small Basic Exam** (35 multiple-choice questions, 65% passing grade).
* **Flight Limits**:
  + Maintain **Visual Line-of-Sight (VLOS)** at all times.
  + Maximum altitude of **122 metres (400 feet)** above ground level.
  + Stay at least **5.6 km** (3 nautical miles) from airports and **1.9 km** (1 nautical mile) from heliports.
  + Stay at least **30 metres horizontally** away from bystanders.  **Summary Table: Weight & Requirements** 

| Feature `[4][5][6]` | Microdrones (< 250g) | Small Drones (250g–25kg) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registration Required** | No | Yes ($5 fee) |
| **Pilot Certificate** | No | Yes (Basic or Advanced) |
| **Minimum Age** | None | 14 (Basic) / 16 (Advanced) |
| **Controlled Airspace** | Prohibited (w/o authorization) | Allowed with Advanced Certificate |
| **Distance from People** | "Safe distance" | At least 30m (Basic) | **Important Tools for 2026** 

* **NAV Drone App**: Use this free app from NAV CANADA to check airspace restrictions and request digital authorization for controlled zones.
* **NRC Drone Site Selection Tool**: A web-based map that identifies where you can and cannot fly based on your certificate level. `[1][2][3]`
