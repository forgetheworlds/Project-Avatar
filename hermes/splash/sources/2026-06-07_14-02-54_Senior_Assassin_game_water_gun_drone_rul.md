**The rules for the 2025–2026 Senior Assassin season** have drastically tightened due to heightened police scrutiny, security concerns, and strict enforcement of the official [Splashin app guidelines](https://splashin.app/safety/elimination-items). `[19][20][21][22][23][24]`

Water Gun Drone Rules 

Using a remote-controlled drone equipped with a water-delivery system is **highly restricted or outright banned** in almost all official leagues due to safety, tracking, and administrative reasons: 

*

* **The "Clear Video" Requirement**: Official rules state that an assassination video must clearly show the water hit **and** the assassin's face in the same frame to prove identity. Drones cannot capture both simultaneously. 

* **Consent and Tracking Constraints**: Platforms like Splashin explicitly prohibit stalking, harassment, or tracking someone via unapproved digital surveillance tools. 

* **Airspace & Property Violations**: Flying drones over private yards or properties without direct permission constitutes a trespassing or privacy violation, resulting in immediate game disqualification. 

* **Exceptions**: A drone drop can only be deemed valid if it is a manual, line-of-sight drop where the assassin is visibly holding the remote in the footage, and the local rulebook has an explicit "air strike" clause. 

*

Campus and Off-Campus Restrictions 

Leagues have strictly updated location boundaries to prevent legal liabilities: 

*

* **School Grounds Banned**: Playing on campus property, at official school events, or during sports practices is an automatic safe zone or grounds for immediate disqualification.

* **Vehicle Zero-Tolerance**: Following severe vehicular accidents in recent seasons, modern rules enforce a **zero-elimination policy** in or around moving vehicles. Cars must be safely parked with windows down or doors open to execute a tag.

* **Workplace & Religious Safety**: Targets are completely immune while on the clock at their jobs, inside commercial businesses (unless direct owner permission is given), and during religious services. 

*

Valid Alternative Elimination Methods 

To bypass the dangers of realistic-looking toy firearms, organizers have introduced safer, weapon-free elimination variations: 

| Elimination Method `[13][14][15][16][17][18]` | Validity & Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **Water Balloons / Bottles** | Widely accepted. Must explode or fully drench the target; a single stray droplet does not count. |
| **The "Spoon" Tag** | Valid when a player tags a target with a standard kitchen spoon. Eliminates weapon-related public panic. |
| **Clothespin / Sticker Tag** | Valid if a player successfully clips a clothespin or places a specific sticker on the target’s back without being noticed. |
| **Sharpie / Marker Mark** | Drawing a swift line on the back of the target's hand or arm. Requires photographic proof of the mark. |
| **The Sock Tag** | Smacking a target with a clean, rolled-up pair of socks. Often used as an indoor alternative. |

2025–2026 Immunity and Purge Mechanics 

*

* **Immunity Items**: Players can secure temporary immunity by wearing visible swim goggles (on their eyes/head) or inflated pool floaties (on their upper left arm). `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`

* **The Purge**: Randomly enforced "Purge Days" automatically deactivate all immunity items (goggles and floaties). During a purge, everyone is fair game, though standard geographical safe zones (like schools) still apply. `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`
