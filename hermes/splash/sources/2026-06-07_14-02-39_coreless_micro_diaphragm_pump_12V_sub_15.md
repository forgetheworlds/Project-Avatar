**Finding a 12V coreless micro diaphragm pump weighing under 15 grams that shoots a 2-meter water stream is mechanically impossible** due to fluid dynamics and hardware constraints. To achieve a 2-meter horizontal stream range (projectile distance), a pump must generate significant pressure and fluid velocity. 

Standard sub-15g micro pumps—such as those manufactured by companies like [BODENFLO](https://bodenpump.com/micro-diaphragm-pump/)—are engineered purely for low-pressure gas sampling or microfluidic transfers. A 12V coreless motor capable of building enough pressure to spray water 2 meters weighs significantly more than 15 grams on its own, before factoring in the weight of the diaphragm housing, valves, and casing. 

To build this DIY drone payload, you must compromise on either your weight budget or your pump architecture.

DIY Nozzle Tuning for Maximum Range 

To achieve your 2-meter stream range using a micro-pump, the nozzle design is just as important as the motor: 

1. **Constrict the Exit**: Do not use a wide tube. Fit a 0.5mm to 1.0mm blunt syringe needle or a 3D-printed cone nozzle onto the outlet tube.
2. **Trade Flow for Velocity**: Constricting the line builds backpressure. It reduces the total volume of water sprayed per minute but dramatically increases the exit velocity, shooting the stream much farther.
