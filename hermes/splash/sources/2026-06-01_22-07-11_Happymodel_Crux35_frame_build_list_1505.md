Critical Build Considerations for 2026 

* **The AIO Selection:** Do not use separate stacks. The[Happymodel Crux35 Frame](https://www.getfpv.com/happymodel-crux35-freestyle-racing-frame-kit.html) has exceptionally tight vertical clearance. A Whoop-style 25.5x25.5mm All-In-One (AIO) board is required. The 20A rated
  CruxF405 AIO handles the amp draw of the 1505 motors on 4S flawlessly without adding the weight of a separate ESC board. 

* **Motor Mounting and KV:** Check your specific 1505 motor choice to confirm it uses a standard **9mm M2 mounting pattern** to match the
  [Crux35 Go to product viewer dialog for this item.](/search?ibp=oshop&prds=pvt:hg,pvo:29,mid:576462859583451787,imageDocid:3969843112451970064,gpcid:14281485122327962982,headlineOfferDocid:8984221076440603155,catalogid:7457016035041253420,productDocid:16225942882417638967,rds:PC_14281485122327962982%7CPROD_PC_14281485122327962982&q=product&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwj8tueivOeUAxUskIkEHRgXGngQxa4PeggIAggACCYQCg) arms. If building for a pure 4S freestyle beast, look for a KV range between **3400KV and 3800KV** to keep motor temperatures and battery sag controlled under full throttle. 

* **FPV System Constraints:** Keeping this build at approximately 193.5g leaves roughly 55g of headroom before hitting the 250g FAA regulatory ceiling. This means you can comfortably upgrade this exact parts list to a digital system—such as the
