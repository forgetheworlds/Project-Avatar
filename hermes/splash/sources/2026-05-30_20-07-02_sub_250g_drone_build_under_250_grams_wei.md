🧠 Weight Reduction Core Strategies 

* **Shave the wires**: Cut power and signal wires to the exact length needed.
* **Remove plastic connectors**: Solder wires directly to the flight controller instead of using heavy plastic plugs.
* **Swap steel for titanium**: Replace structural steel frame screws with titanium or aluminum hardware.
* **Ditch the TPU**: Eliminate or minimize bulky 3D-printed GoPro and antenna mounts.
* **Use tape or rubber bands**: Secure receivers and antennas with electrical tape instead of heavy plastic mounts.
* **Exposed electronics**: Strip plastic casings or heat-shrink tubing off video transmitters and receivers. `[4][5][6]`
