Important Print Safety & Design Tips 

*

* Avoid using standard **PLA filament**, as it lacks the temperature resistance required to withstand high-current motor heat transfer.

* Ensure your **motor leads** are cut with comfortable clearance, as threading wires through narrow 6mm hollow tubes requires neat, insulation-wrapped soldering steps.

* Run a complete **dry fit sequence** before applying your permanent structural epoxy or retaining compound to the carbon-to-plastic contact points.
