The **Hilintec S18 micro diaphragm liquid pump** is a compact, cost-effective fluid transmission solution optimized for tight spaces. 

Motor Options 

The S18 series features two distinct motor variants tailored to different operational needs: 

*

* **Brushless DC (BLDC) Motor (BL Type)**: Designed for long life, high reliability, and scenarios that require continuous operation.

* **Brushed DC Motor (BA Type)**: Suited for cost-sensitive applications, high-pressure output, and high water head demands. 

*

Dimensions & Weight 

Physical specifications change depending on the integrated motor configuration: 

*

* **BLDC Motor Dimensions**: Approximately **43 × 25 × 41 mm**.

* **Brushed Motor Dimensions**: Approximately **55 × 25 × 41 mm**.

* **Form Factor**: Features an extremely low profile designed for minimal installation footprints. 

*

Key Specifications & Datasheet Highlights 

*

* **Power Supply Options**: Supports **5V DC** or **12V DC** inputs.

* **Acoustics & Vibration**: Engineered for extremely low noise, with the S18M variant dropping as low as **44 decibels**.

* **Material Customization**: Pump heads can be ordered in corrosion-resistant engineering plastics like **PPS** or **PVDF**.

* **Seals & Diaphragms**: Component options include **EPDM, FKM, or PTFE** to match chemical compatibility with organic solvents, strong acids, and alkalis. 

*
