The actual measured weight of a standard **12V 370 micro diaphragm water pump** typically ranges from **60 to 66 grams**. While some minimal variations exist based on the specific wire harness or casing options, the bare pump configuration sits firmly in this ultra-lightweight tier. 

This extremely low mass profile makes it an ideal component for micro-drone payloads (such as miniature agricultural sprayers, localized environmental sampling setups, or automated fluid-dispensing mechanisms) where every single gram impacts flight time and battery efficiency.

Drone Integration Tips 

* **Vibration Isolation**: Diaphragm pumps pulse mechanically. Direct-mounting to a carbon fiber drone frame will amplify high-frequency noise and can mess with flight controller gyros. Wrap the pump body in thin open-cell foam or silicone sleeves before securing it.
