**Senior Assassins** games generally run from **April through June**, aligning with the final months of the spring semester before graduation. Registration and target assignments typically take place in April, with gameplay intensifying through May and wrapping up by early June. 

📅 General 2026 Game Schedule `[19][20][21][22][23][24]`

Because Senior Assassins is entirely student-run and not sponsored by universities, specific timelines depend on individual student councils or game admins. However, most schools follow this standard phases framework: 

*

* **April (Registration & Setup)**: Sign-ups open, entry fees are collected via Venmo or e-transfer, and profiles are generated. 

* **May (Active Gameplay & Rounds)**: The game officially launches early in the month. Rounds typically reset every 7 days, requiring players to eliminate at least one target per week to stay alive. 

* **Late May / Early June (The Purge & Finals)**: To force a conclusion before graduation, "Purge Days" are called. During a purge, safety items are nullified. Games are temporarily paused for official commencement ceremonies and resume immediately after until a final winner stands.

📱 Tracking Apps: Splashin vs. Life360 `[13][14][15][16][17][18]`

While some informal games utilize a shared **Life360** circle code for manual location tracking, the vast majority of university games utilize dedicated apps like **Splashin**.

🛑 Official Game Rules & Campus Safe Zones 

Violating basic safety or zone rules results in immediate disqualification or removal from the prize pool. `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`

*

* **Weapons**: Only standard, non-modified, brightly-colored plastic water guns are permitted. Realistic-looking imitation firearms, Orbeez, or pellet guns are strictly banned due to extreme police safety concerns.

* **Campus Safe Zones**: No eliminations are allowed inside university buildings, classrooms, libraries, or dining facilities. School-sanctioned events (sports practices, club meetings, and exams) are entirely off-limits.

* **Off-Campus Safe Zones**: Places of worship, workplaces, and active vehicles (no tagging while driving) are universal safe zones.

* **Immunity Gear**: In most rulesets, wearing a pair of goggles or inflatable pool floaties visibly around your neck or head provides temporary immunity from assassination during standard rounds.

* **Proof**: Every successful splash must be caught clearly on video to be approved by an administrator.
