For a tiny drone water gun or payload delivery system, sourcing a **12V, self-priming water pump weighing under 20 grams with a 500 mL/min flow rate** requires balancing highly specific micro-fluidic technologies. 

A direct breakdown of your specified categories alongside the best high-utility alternatives reveals how these specifications intersect for drone integration. 

---

Core Technology Breakdown 

1. Micro Diaphragm Pumps (S18 & Similar) 

Standard mini diaphragm pumps like the **S18 Diaphragm Mini Liquid Pump** are excellent for self-priming and continuous pressure. However, standard models usually weigh **40g to 65g** (such as the

* **The Exception:** Ultra-micro variants engineered by brands like [BODENFLO](https://bodenpump.com/micro-diaphragm-pump/) use specialized micro-motors to bring the weight down to a highly competitive **18 grams** while maintaining a liquid flow rate range of 300–1600 mL/min. 

2. Piezoelectric Micro Pumps `[31][32][33][34][35][36]`

If your priority is keeping the payload strictly **sub-10g to 20g**, piezoelectric pumps (like the [Takasago SDMP320](https://www.sandur.net/piezoelectric-micro-pumps.php) or CurieJet PS series) are the lightest options available. 

* **The Drone Problem:** Piezoelectric pumps rely on a high-frequency vibrating crystal to push fluid. While they are incredibly lightweight (approx. 9g) and can self-prime, their high-flow versions max out around **20–25 mL/min**. They **cannot** reach your required 500 mL/min target. Furthermore, they require dedicated AC/high-voltage pulse driver boards rather than a native 12V DC input. 

3. Coreless Motor Pumps `[25][26][27][28][29][30]`

Pumps utilizing a **coreless DC motor** feature a rotor that lacks an iron core, which drastically drops the overall weight, eliminates magnetic cogging, and allows for rapid spin-up times. Custom-assembled micro gear or micro centrifugal pumps built around a coreless motor offer the best power-to-weight ratio for a drone water gun, pushing high volumes while remaining under the 20g threshold. 

---

Highly Viable Tiny Drone Alternatives 

To achieve a true **500 mL/min flow rate at under 20 grams**, standard commercial off-the-shelf 12V household pumps are generally too heavy. Instead, look to these specialized drone-weight alternatives: `[19][20][21][22][23][24]`

* **Micro Coreless Gear Pumps (12V Customized)**:
  + Often sourced via specialized RC or microfluidic suppliers, these units mate a 10mm or 12mm coreless motor to a tiny plastic gear housing.
  + **Pros:** Weighs roughly 12–15g; easily achieves 400–600 mL/min; provides a sharp, pressurized stream ideal for a water gun shooter.
  + **Cons:** Weak dry self-priming; requires the water reservoir to be positioned level with or slightly above the pump inlet. `[13][14][15][16][17][18]`
* **Micro RC Fuel Pumps (Brushed/Coreless 12V)**:
  + Designed for nitro/gas RC model airplanes and drones to transfer fuel.
  + **Pros:** Highly optimized for low weight (typically 15g–22g) and self-priming. They regularly hit 500+ mL/min at 12V.
  + **Cons:** Gears are often optimized for fuel lubrication, so running pure water may reduce the lifespan compared to a dedicated water diaphragm. `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`
* **Custom 3D-Printed Peristaltic/Centrifugal + Coreless Servo Motor**:
  + A common approach in the custom drone community. By pairing a high-RPM micro coreless motor (from a tiny toothpick drone) with a tiny 3D-printed centrifugal housing, you can build a system weighing under 10 grams.
  + **Pros:** Total weight control; easily blasts 500 mL/min+.
  + **Cons:** Zero self-priming capability; must be gravity-fed from the bottom of your drone's water tank.
