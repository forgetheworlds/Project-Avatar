🚀 Sub-250g Build Strategy `[7][8][9][10][11][12]`

Because the base dry weight sits well under 100 grams, keeping the drone sub-250g is incredibly easy. This massive weight surplus allows you to prioritize flight dynamics, flight times, or heavy camera systems: `[1][2][3][4][5][6]`

* **Ultra-light Toothpick Spec (Target: ~125g All-Up Weight):** Pair the dry frame with a small **3S 500mAh to 550mAh LiPo** (which adds roughly 43g). This results in an incredibly nimble quad that acts like an oversized whoop with 6 to 8 minutes of casual backyard flight time. 
* **Freestyle Powerhouse Spec (Target: ~170g–190g All-Up Weight):** Run slightly larger 1303.5 or 1404 motors on a **4S 650mAh to 850mAh battery** (approx. 65g–88g). This yields high throttle authority and handles wind tracking much better without ever threatening the 250g legal boundary. 

* **Digital HD/Cinewhoop Spec (Target: ~150g–180g All-Up Weight):** Swap the analog FPV stack for digital video transmitters like the
