## FACT-055: 4S Li-Ion RULED OUT — Lipo ONLY FOR SPLASH
**Severity: HIGH | Category: Flight Hardware**
4S Li-Ion pack (cylindrical 18650 cells) weighs ~200g — >50% of Splash's total usable payload budget. Conclusively ruled out. Discharge rating (10-15A) also insufficient for 1505 3800KV motors (20.5A peak each). 4S 850mAh LiPo (95-105g) or LiHV (85-88g) is the only viable battery. Recommended: Gaoneng GNB 4S 850mAh 120C HV at 88g (saves 14g vs Tattu R-Line 95C at 102g). Flight time: 6-8 min cruising / 3-5 min aggressive. Power budget: ~2.12A cruise draw, 850mAh at 80% DoD = 680mAh usable = ~19 min theoretical / 6-8 min real-world.
**Action:** Order Gaoneng GNB 4S 850mAh 120C HV or Tattu R-Line 4S 850mAh 95C as backup.
