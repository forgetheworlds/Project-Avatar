## FACT-030: COMPETITOR ANALYSIS — 4 CRITICAL FAILURE MODES DOCUMENTED
**Severity: CRITICAL | Category: Build Preparation**
Community builds (Reddit, YouTube, Hackaday) reveal 4 critical failure modes for drone water gun builds: (1) Sloshing Effect — open water tanks cause CG shift and PID overcorrection; solution: baffles or medical IV bag (recommended). (2) Newton's Recoil — water jet pushes drone nose down; solution: nozzle at true CoG + PID compensation. (3) Voltage Brownouts — pump on flight battery causes FC reboot; solution: dedicated BEC or separate 2S battery (Splash architecture confirmed correct). (4) Weight Math Trap — over-engineering leaves no water payload margin; target dry weight < 180g for 70g fluid margin. Key reference projects: jonathanrandall/electric_watergun_with_tracking (direct water gun + ESP32-CAM), apssouza22/smart-drone (person tracking), maxboels/TrackingPanTiltCam (turret mode).
**Action:** Adopt medical IV bag for zero-free-surface reservoir. Mount nozzle at true CoG. Maintain separate 2S payload battery. Target dry weight < 180g.
