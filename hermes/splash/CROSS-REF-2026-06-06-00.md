## Key Findings
1. **🔴 Pump weight crisis** — 385 pump at 110g exceeds 50g payload. 370 at 60-65g is tight. Coreless gear pumps (12-15g) are only path to sub-250g but need testing.
2. **🟢 MG90S definitive choice** — Metal gears + ball bearing essential for recoil. Run at 6V via separate BEC.
3. **🔴 MOSFET correction** — IRFZ44N NOT suitable for 3.3/5V GPIO. Must use logic-level IRL3705N/IRLZ44N.
4. **🟢 Flexible IV bag wins** — 1-2g, zero slosh, perfect CG. Beats rigid tank and syringe alternatives.
5. **🟢 Splash is first-in-class** — No existing sub-250g drone with water projectile capability found.
