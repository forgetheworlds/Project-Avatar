### 2.3 Propeller Confirmation

Prior research used HQProp T3.5x2x3. Google AI Mode data anchors on **HQProp T3.5x2.5x3** which provides:
- Higher pitch (2.5 vs 2.0) = more aggressive thrust response
- Better for freestyle maneuvers and quick altitude changes
- Slightly lower efficiency at cruise than T3.5x2x3
- Acceptable tradeoff for Splash's need to quickly change altitude during protection mode orbits

**Decision:** Use HQProp T3.5x2.5x3 for Splash. The higher pitch allows faster altitude recovery after firing recoil events. The thrust margin (8:1) is sufficient.

### 2.4 Impact on PID Tuning

Prior research stated "P-gain -20%, D-term +15%" for 1505 motors. New data confirms:
- 1505 stator has more copper mass than 1404 — higher torque inertia
- Larger Kd values possible (counteract momentum during rapid direction reversals)
- Filter: LPF alpha ≈ 0.15 (match MG90S servo response)

### 4.3 Critical Limitation: Tiny Antenna = Fewer Satellites

The GM10 Nano's miniature patch antenna (optimized for <3" frames) captures significantly fewer satellites than a full-sized M10 GPS module (e.g., GepRC M10 or BZGNSS P18).

| Metric | GM10 Nano V3 | Full-size M10 Module | Impact |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|--------|
| Satellites locked | 14-22 | 30+ | GM10 has 30-50% fewer |
| Weak signal handling | Average | Excellent | GM10 drops under canopy |
| RF interference shielding | Minimal (copper foil) | Active shielding | Sensitive to VTX/ESC noise |
| Best use | Open field, short-range | All conditions | Splash OK for open field |

**Implication for Splash:** The GM10 Nano is **sufficient** for Senior Assassins play (open courtyards, fields). It provides adequate GPS accuracy (1.5-2.0m) for orbit waypoints and return-to-home. However:
- Avoid flying under heavy tree canopy (GPS may lose lock)
- Keep VTX at reasonable power (25-200mW) to minimize RF interference
- Use compass version for accurate heading (critical for orbit mode)

## §5. 🟢 4S Battery Verdict: LiPo vs Li-Ion — Conclusive

### 5.1 Li-Ion Ruled OUT

| Factor | 4S 850mAh LiPo | 4S Li-Ion (18650) |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Weight** | **95-105g** | ~200g (4× cells + wrapping) |
| **Discharge** | 60-120C continuous | 10-15A max (sags hard) |
| **Flight time** | 6-8 min cruising | 20-25 min (but sluggish) |
| **Sub-250g possible?** | ✅ Tight squeeze (~245g) | ❌ Impossible (350g+) |
| **Punch-outs** | Excellent | Voltage sag → drop out of air |
| **Splash suitability** | ✅ Perfect | ❌ Too heavy, too weak |

**Verdict:** Li-Ion is conclusively ruled out for Splash. The 200g pack weight alone exceeds available payload budget.

### 5.2 Recommended LiPo Options

| Battery | Type | Weight | C-Rating | Flight Time | Price | Notes |
|---------|------|--------|----------|-------------|-------|-------|
| **Tattu R-Line 850mAh 4S** | LiPo | ~102g | 95C | 6-8 min cruise | Industry standard, reliable |
| **Gaoneng GNB 850mAh 4S HV** | LiHV | **~88g** | 120C | 7-9 min cruise | **Best weight savings** |
| **Sub250 720mAh 4S LiHV** | LiHV | ~68g | 100C | 5 min | Ultra-light, for tight budgets |

**Recommendation:** Gaoneng GNB 4S 850mAh 120C HV for Splash. The 88g weight saves ~14g vs Tattu, and the 120C discharge rating provides ample headroom for 1505 motors (peak 20.5A each, ~82A system). HV (4.35V/cell) adds extra initial punch.
