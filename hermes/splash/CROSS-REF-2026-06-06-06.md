## Cross-Project Insights for Avatar
- **ByteTrack > BoT-SORT paradigm**: For low-velocity aerial platforms (orbit/loiter < 2 m/s), ByteTrack's speed advantage outweighs BoT-SORT's motion compensation. This generalizes to ANY drone CV project where the platform moves slowly.
- **Geometric depth from bounding box**: Nearly-zero-overhead distance estimation for known-size objects (humans, cars) — eliminates need for depth ML models in constrained scenarios.
- **ESP32-S3 inference ceiling**: Confirmed that ESP32-S3 cannot replace even a low-end companion computer for real-time YOLO. Projects should budget for a separate compute node (MacBook/Jetson/RPi).
- **Direct GPIO PWM > I2C servo controllers**: For <4 servo projects, ESP32 LEDC peripheral is strictly superior to PCA9685 — lower jitter, zero latency, no added hardware.
