### F-2026-06-06-099 [HIGH] — Direct ESP32 GPIO PWM Superior to PCA9685 for Splash
For 2-servo (pan/tilt) setup, direct ESP32 GPIO PWM (LEDC peripheral) is strictly superior to PCA9685 I2C controller. ESP32: <50ns jitter (crystal-locked), up to 16-bit resolution, zero latency (hardware register write). PCA9685: ±5-25µs jitter (4% RC oscillator drift), 12-bit only, 200-500µs I2C latency per write. For Splash: 2 servos, ESP32 has 16 PWM channels natively. PCA9685 adds 2.5g weight and $3 cost with NO benefit — worse signal quality.
**Action:** Wire MG90S servos directly to ESP32 GPIO18 (pan) and GPIO19 (tilt). Use 6V BEC for servo power. No PCA9685.
