## 1. 🟢 D5 DECISION: MICRO DIAPHRAGM PUMP > SPRING-PRESSURIZED SYRINGE

### Direct Comparison (Google AI Mode, May 2026)

| Feature | Spring-Pressurized Syringe | Micro Diaphragm Pump |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| **Flow Duration** | Intermittent (limited by syringe volume) | Continuous (limited only by tank size) |
| **Pressure Profile** | Decays linearly (Hooke's Law: F = kx) | Constant throughout operation |
| **Weight Distribution** | Dynamic CG shift as plunger moves | Static (liquid drains evenly) |
| **Mechanical Complexity** | High (spring, linkages, high-torque servo) | Low (purely electrical switching) |
| **Self-Priming** | No (must be manually filled) | Yes (self-priming diaphragm) |
| **Refilling** | Manual reset or complex reverse mechanics | Simple gravity feed from reservoir |
| **Recoil Profile** | Single high-impulse spike per shot | Smooth, controllable stream |

### Why Diaphragm Pump Wins for Splash

The spring syringe's critical weakness is **pressure decay** — the exit velocity drops as the spring expands, causing the stream to droop toward the end of the stroke. The structural load on the drone chassis from compressed spring stress and sudden recoil is also higher.

The micro diaphragm pump delivers **constant pressure** from start to finish, uniform stream trajectory, simpler mounting, and self-priming operation.

**Decision verdict**: Adopt micro diaphragm pump. But correct PUMP CLASS is critical — see §1a.

## 1a. ⚠️ CRITICAL PUMP CLASS CORRECTION

### The Error
Google AI Mode returned specs for the **030 series micro diaphragm pump** (3-5V, 14-16g, 80-150 ml/min). The table claimed "20-30 PSI at 5V" alongside "Max Head Pressure: ~1.5 Meters." These figures are **contradictory** — 1.5m head = 2.1 PSI, not 20-30 PSI. The 20-30 PSI figure is dead-head pressure at zero flow (stall pressure of the diaphragm). The **actual delivery pressure at useful flow rates** is ~2 PSI (1.5m head).

### Physics Check: 030 Pump Through 1.0mm Nozzle
At 2 PSI (14 kPa):
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Max theoretical velocity | sqrt(2 × 14,000 / 1000) = 5.3 m/s |
| Natural flow through 1.0mm orifice | 5.3 × π × (0.0005)² = 250 ml/min |
| Pump max flow (030) | 80-150 ml/min — limits actual flow |
| Actual exit velocity (pump-limited) | ~3-4 m/s |
| Effective range from 2m altitude | **~1.5-2m** |
| Stream coherence | Poor at low velocity — breaks up before target |

**Conclusion: 030 pump is TOO WEAK for Splash's requirement.**

### The Correct Pump Class: 12V 370/385 Micro Diaphragm
The established Splash spec from May 22/29 nightly research targets **12V micro diaphragm pumps** (370/385 motor class):

| Parameter | 370/385 Pump (Correct) | 030 Pump (Rejected) |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Motor** | 370/385 brushed | 030 coreless |
| **Voltage** | 6-12V | 3-5V |
| **Flow Rate** | **0.8-1.5 L/min** (800-1500 ml/min) | 80-150 ml/min |
| **Pressure** | **0.3-0.8 bar (4-12 PSI)** | ~2 PSI (useful) |
| **Weight** | 15-25g | 14-16g |
| **Inrush** | 2-3A peak | 0.1-0.6A |
| **Cost** | $5-8 AliExpress | $3-7 AliExpress |

### Why 12V is Acceptable for Splash
- **Two-battery architecture**: 4S LiPo (flight) + 2S 300mAh (payload) — pump runs from payload battery
- If running from 4S: Step-down regulator (LM2596) to 12V, or use BEC
- If running from 2S (7.4V): Pump operates at reduced flow (~0.5-0.8 L/min at ~3-5 PSI) — acceptable
- **Recommended**: Dedicated 12V step-down from 4S flight battery, avoiding separate payload battery weight

### Corrected D5 Decision
**Adopt 12V 370/385 micro diaphragm pump** (e.g., typical AliExpress 12V micro diaphragm water pump, 0.8-1.5 L/min, 15-25g). Reject 030 series as underpowered. Spring syringe remains the backup option for ultra-lightweight builds but not recommended.

## 3. 🟢 SERVO PINCH VALVE CONFIRMED

### Comparison: Pinch Valve vs Ball Valve

| Component | Servo Pinch Valve | Motorized Ball Valve |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Actuator** | MG90S servo (9-13g) | Integrated DC motor + heavy gearbox |
| **Valve Body** | Silicone tube (existing fluid line) | Brass/stainless micro-housing |
| **Mount** | Minimalist 3D-printed PETG cam | Robust frame for torque reaction |
| **Connectors** | Direct pass-through | NPT/barbed metal adapters + sealant |
| **Total Weight** | **~10g** | **50g+** |
| **Corrosion Risk** | None (fluid isolated in silicone tube) | High (metal body + seals) |
| **Clog Risk** | Low (silicone tube is smooth bore) | High (internal particulate jams) |

### Mechanism
- 3D-printed eccentric cam on servo horn
- When rotated, cam pinches silicone tube against fixed wall
- MG90S stall torque (~1.8 kg·cm) sufficient for up to 30 PSI tube pressure
- External silicone tube diameter: ~5mm (3mm ID) — standard for 030 pump ports

### Splash Implementation
- Use **MG90S** (metal gear, 13g) for reliability over SG90 (plastic gear, 9g)
- 3D-printed cam mechanism in PETG
- Silicone tubing: 3mm ID × 5mm OD (food-grade silicone)
- Tube runs from pump → pinch valve → nozzle (continuous, no joints)

## 5. 🟢 WATERPROOFING PROCEDURE

### Material: MG Chemicals 422B Silicone Conformal Coating

Industry standard for FPV drone waterproofing. Cures flexible, -40°C to 200°C range, UV indicator for verification.

### Application Steps
1. **Clean**: 99% isopropyl alcohol on FC, ESC, RX — remove flux and dirt
2. **Mask**: Tape over connectors, USB ports, barometer (tiny hole), boot buttons
3. **First layer**: Fine-tip nylon brush, thin even coat across PCB
4. **Cure**: 10-15 minutes until tack-free
5. **Second layer**: Apply perpendicular to first layer (eliminates pinholes)
6. **Check**: UV blacklight — 422B glows where applied
7. **Third layer** (optional): Extra protection for capacitor legs and solder joints
8. **Final cure**: 24 hours at room temperature before powering

### Splash-Specific Protection
- **Capacitor legs**: Extra coating on high-voltage exposed leads
- **XT60 power leads**: Fully encapsulate solder joints
- **Motor pads**: Ensure full coverage on ESC motor solder pads
- **Payload area**: Conformal coat FC + ESC even if payload battery is separate
- **Hawkeye camera**: Do NOT coat — keep lens, ports, vents clear
- **ESP32**: Conformal coat carefully, keep USB port and antenna connector clear

### Coating Exclusion Zones
| Component | Why | Action |
|-----------|-----|--------|
| **Barometer** | Tiny hole for pressure sensing | Mask with kapton tape |
| **USB ports** | Coating insulates data pins | Mask or leave clear |
| **Connectors** | Creates intermittent connections | Don't coat pins inside plugs |
| **Boot buttons** | Glues switches shut | Mask |
| **Camera lens** | Optical clarity | Keep clear |
