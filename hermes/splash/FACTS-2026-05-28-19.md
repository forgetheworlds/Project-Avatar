## FACT-001: YOLO MODEL SELECTION
YOLOv11n (CoreML) is THE detection backbone for Splash. 125-145 FPS on MacBook M3 Apple Neural Engine. 5.4MB (2.6M params). +2.2% mAP over YOLOv8n. C2PSA attention block improves occlusion handling — critical for tracking people through trees. Export to .mlpackage for ANE optimization. Source: Google AI Mode + Ultralytics docs comparison.

## FACT-002: MULTI-PERSON TRACKER
ByteTrack is the superior tracker for drone top-down views. Outperforms DeepSORT (appearance Re-ID fails from above) and StrongSORT (too slow). Keeps low-confidence detections during occlusion — critical for maintaining ID through trees. IoU matching only — negligible compute overhead. Priority score function: P = w1·Sconf + w2·(1-Dcenter) + w3·IIoU. Source: Google AI Mode + Taskmonk tracking comparison 2025.

## FACT-003: REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATION
maxboels/TrackingPanTiltCam is THE open-source reference for Splash auto-aim. YOLOv8 + Kalman filter + PCA9685 servo turret with motion compensation. Two modes: Surveillance (smooth) and Turret (precision targeting — exactly what Splash needs). Adaptable: swap YOLOv8→YOLOv11n, MG996R→MG90S, Jetson→MacBook M3. Source: GitHub repo full documentation fetch.

## FACT-004: MOTION COMPENSATION
Three-layer motion compensation required to prevent servo feedback loops on a moving drone: (1) IMU gyro feedforward calculates expected visual shift from camera rotation, (2) Optical flow subtraction isolates target motion from ego-motion (F_target = F_total - F_ego), (3) Hysteresis deadband prevents servo jitter from sub-pixel noise. Without this, pan-tilt tracking oscillates uncontrollably. Source: Google AI Mode + maxboels implementation.

## FACT-005: TEAM IDENTIFICATION (HSV)
HSV color filtering with adaptive thresholding + CLAHE preprocessing + histogram backprojection provides robust team identification from drone footage. Red requires dual masks (H: 0-10 and 170-180). Blue is single range (H: 100-130). Bhattacharyya distance < 0.35 for re-identification after occlusion. Adaptive V_min based on frame average brightness handles outdoor light changes. Source: Google AI Mode.

## FACT-006: DEPTH/RANGE FINDING
Depth Anything V2 (Small, 25M params) is the best monocular depth model for drones but too heavy for real-time alongside YOLO. Splash uses bounding box size heuristic (distance ≈ k / bbox_width) + VL53L1X ToF sensor (<4m accurate range) for firing decisions. VL53L1X already planned from May 22 optical flow research (RNGFND1_TYPE=36). Source: Google AI Mode + cross-reference with May 22 research.

## FACT-007: ESP32 CAMERA STRATEGY
ESP32-CAM (OV2640) streams MJPEG over WiFi to MacBook M3 ground station. ESP32-S3 cannot run YOLO (SRAM too small). MediaPipe Face Detection is unsuitable for drone altitudes (requires 1-2m range). YOLO whole-body detection from MacBook is the correct approach. Estimated pipeline latency: ~12ms total (75-80 FPS). Source: Google AI Mode + Jonathan Randall water gun project.
