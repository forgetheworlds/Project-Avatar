### 2b. HSV Color Filtering — Team Jersey Identification (Red vs Blue)

**Complete implementation pipeline:**

1. **YOLO Person Detection** — Filter by class ID 0 (person)
2. **Torso Region-of-Interest** — Crop 25% to 65% of bbox height (isolates jersey)
3. **HSV Color Space** — BGR→HSV separates hue from intensity
4. **Dual Red Mask** — Red wraps around 0° and 180° in HSV:
   - Lower Red 1: H[0-10], S[70-255], V[50-255]
   - Lower Red 2: H[170-180], S[70-255], V[50-255]
   - Combined: `mask_red = cv2.bitwise_or(mask_red1, mask_red2)`
5. **Blue Mask** — H[100-140], S[70-255], V[50-255]
6. **Pixel Count** — `cv2.countNonZero(mask)` with min_pixel_threshold=50
7. **Team Classification** — Dominant color wins

**HSV ranges verified for outdoor conditions** — Saturation floor of 70 eliminates white/black, Value floor of 50 eliminates darkness. Provides stability under shadows and varying lighting.

**Source:** Google AI Mode (HSV filtering)

### 2d. Monocular Depth Estimation for Range Finding

**Formula:** `D = (F × H_actual) / H_pixels`
- F = camera focal length (pixels, e.g., 650 for typical drone cam)
- H_actual = 1.70m (average human height)
- H_pixels = bounding box height from YOLO

**Performance:** 60+ FPS on edge processors. No deep learning depth model needed.

**Advanced PnP for pitch/roll compensation** — Use MediaPipe pose landmarks (Head, Shoulders, Feet) for 4-point PnP via `cv2.solvePnP()`:

```python
object_points = np.array([[0.0, 0.85, 0.0], [-0.25, 0.60, 0.0], 
                          [0.25, 0.60, 0.0], [0.0, -0.85, 0.0]])
success, rvec, tvec = cv2.solvePnP(object_points, image_points, 
                                    camera_matrix, dist_coeffs)
distance_pnp = np.linalg.norm(tvec)
```

**Geofence classification:** SAFE (>5m) → WARNING (3-5m) → CRITICAL (<3m). 1m hysteresis zone for boundary ambiguity.

### 2e. Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow + Kalman Filter Fusion

**Architecture:**
1. YOLO detects person → returns bounding box
2. `cv2.goodFeaturesToTrack()` samples 20 keypoints inside bbox
3. `cv2.calcOpticalFlowPyrLK()` tracks keypoints frame-to-frame
4. Kalman Filter (4 states: x, y, dx, dy) smooths and predicts
5. On occlusion: Kalman prediction maintains track using historical velocity

**Kalman parameters:** processNoiseCov=0.03, measurementNoiseCov=0.5, winSize=(15,15), maxLevel=2

**Fallback behavior:** If LK drops all points (occlusion), falls back to Kalman Predict phase only. Reinitializes when YOLO redetects.

**Source:** Google AI Mode (Lucas-Kanade + Kalman)

### 3a. Ballistic Trajectory Calculations

For downward spray from drone at altitude h:
- Fall time: `t = sqrt(2h/g)`
- Drone forward speed: v0 (e.g., 5.3 m/s during orbit)
- Lead distance: `Δx = v0 × t`
- At h=2m: t=0.64s, Δx=3.4m
- At h=3m: t=0.78s, Δx=4.1m
- At h=5m: t=1.01s, Δx=5.4m

**Key insight:** Lead distance scales with drone's horizontal speed. During orbit at ~2-5 m/s, compensate by aiming ahead of target. During hover (zero horizontal speed), no lead needed.

**385 motor pumps** deliver 0.8-1.5 L/min at 0.3-0.8 bar — providing effective spray range of 2-3m from 2m altitude.

## HOUR 7: COMPETITOR ANALYSIS — FULL COVERAGE

### 7a. Four Critical Failure Modes from Community Builds

**1. THE SLOSHING EFFECT — Death of Flight Stability**
- Open water tanks cause instant wobble on pitch
- Center of Gravity shifts dynamically → PID loop overcorrects
- **Solution:** Internal baffles (walls with small holes) OR flexible medical fluid bladders that collapse as fluid is spent
- Medical IV bags are the best option — zero free-surface effect

**2. NEWTON'S THIRD LAW — Water Jet Recoil**
- Spray forces micro-drone backwards
- On sub-250g, this kinetic force pushes nose down
- **Solution:** Mount nozzle at true CoG + aggressive ArduPilot PID tuning
- Recoil counter-yaw mixer (prior fact F-2026-05-30-001) confirmed required

**3. VOLTAGE SAGS AND BROWNOUTS**
- Tapping main flight battery for pump causes voltage drop
- FC reboots mid-air when pump triggers
- **Solution:** Dedicated BEC or separate payload battery
- Splash's separate 2S 300mAh is CORRECT

**4. THE WEIGHT MATH TRAP**
- Over-engineering frame + pan-tilt servos pushes dry weight past 200g
- Leaves room for only drops of water
- **Solution:** Fix nozzle to frame front. Let drone yaw/pitch aim. Target dry weight < 180g.
- Splash 315g AUW with 350g thrust margin is tight but viable

**Source:** Google AI Mode (competitor analysis), Reddit (r/diydrones, r/fpv, r/drones), Hackaday, YouTube
