## New Facts Added (5)

### F-2026-05-29-015 [CRITICAL] Pump Physics Wall — Spring Syringe Required
Sub-15g diaphragm pumps (DP-6 Micro 030) max out at 0.04–0.08 L/min — physically impossible to generate useful water gun stream. Pumps capable of 0.5–2.0 L/min weigh 65–110g (Micro 370, R385). The 50g payload budget cannot accommodate an electric pump. RECOMMENDED: Spring-loaded 20mL syringe + servo pinch valve (total ~30g). Eliminates pump weight, MOSFET circuit, brownout risk, and slosh simultaneously.
→ **ACTION: Build spring-syringe prototype. Test stream range at varying spring forces. Verify servo pinch valve seals at 2-5 PSI.**

### F-2026-05-29-016 [HIGH] Off-Shelf Nozzle: 3D Printer Brass M6
Standard V6/Volcano 3D printer brass nozzles (M6 thread, 1.0mm orifice) make perfect water gun tips — pre-manufactured internal conical taper, knife-edge exit, $0.50 each. Integrate into 3D-printed PETG housing with laminar flow straightener (micro-channel matrix → settling zone → sharp orifice). Print vertical orientation, coat interior with epoxy for FDM ridge smoothing. 1.0mm orifice at 2-3 PSI from spring syringe → estimated 3m range.
→ **ACTION: Design nozzle housing in Fusion 360. Print PETG, test with spring syringe. Measure stream coherence at 2m, 3m, 5m.**

### F-2026-05-29-017 [CRITICAL] IRFZ44N ≠ ESP32 Compatible — Use Servo Pinch Instead
IRFZ44N requires 10V gate drive for full saturation. At 3.3V (ESP32 GPIO), it barely conducts — massive heat, voltage drop, likely failure. Even IRLZ44N (logic-level) is marginal at 3.3V. Documented case of ESP32 destroyed by improper MOSFET pump switching. The spring-syringe + servo pinch approach eliminates MOSFET entirely — valve is controlled via PCA9685 I2C PWM, no gate drive, no flyback diode, no brownout risk.
→ **ACTION: If pump-based approach is ever revived, use IRLZ44N + gate driver IC. For spring-syringe, PCA9685 directly controls pinch servo.**

### F-2026-05-29-018 [HIGH] MG90S Metal Gear Mandatory — SG90 Will Strip
SG90 (9g, plastic gears, 1.8 kg·cm) vs MG90S (13.4g, metal gears, 2.2 kg·cm). The 4.4g penalty is non-negotiable — every successful water turret project uses metal gear servos. Plastic SG90 gears strip under water gun payload weight. 3D printed pan-tilt assemblies exist for MG90S (jpralves.net, Printables). Splash needs 2× MG90S (pan + tilt, ~27g) + 1× SG90 (pinch valve, 9g — low load, plastic OK here).
→ **ACTION: Order 2× MG90S + 1× SG90. Print pan-tilt bracket. Test full sweep range with nozzle + tubing weight.**

### F-2026-05-29-019 [HIGH] Sub-250g at Risk — 270g AUW with Payload
Recalculated AUW with lightweight tube frame (17g) + water gun payload (70g) + 4S 650mAh (75g) = 269.8g — 20g over limit. Path to compliance: 4S 450mAh (50g, saves 25g), or remove Hawkeye Thumb (17g), or lighter 1404 motors (saves ~10g). Spring-syringe approach saves 35-40g vs pump-based water gun. Accept 260-270g as "close enough" if strict compliance not enforced.
→ **ACTION: Weigh all components on precision scale. Order 4S 450mAh test battery. Decide: strict sub-250g or accept 260-270g.**
