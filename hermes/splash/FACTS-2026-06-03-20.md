### F-2026-06-03-057 [CRITICAL] — YOLO26n NMS-Free
YOLO26n uses end-to-end bipartite matching (no NMS). On MacBook M3 ANE: 9-11ms inference (100-110 FPS). With ByteTrack: 75-85 FPS. With Kalman-only: 90-95 FPS sustained. Supersedes YOLO11n. Export: `model.export(format='coreml', nms=False, half=True)`.
**Action:** Upgrade CV pipeline to YOLO26n. Update export command, test integrated pipeline.

### F-2026-06-03-058 [CRITICAL] — ArduPilot Harmonic Notch Mandatory
Sub-250g micro-drones with 3800KV motors MUST use harmonic notch filter or instant flyaway. Config: `INS_HNOTCH_ENABLE=1, INS_HNOTCH_MODE=3, INS_HNOTCH_HMNCS=7`. Requires ESC telemetry connection to FC UART RX.
**Action:** Add to Splash ArduPilot params. Connect ESC telemetry. Test in SITL.

### F-2026-06-03-059 [HIGH] — Water Stream Ballistics for Splash
S18 pump (0.3-0.8 bar, 2mm nozzle): exit velocity 5.3-7.1 m/s. Effective range just 2-4m (40% shorter than ideal due to droplet drag). Drop comp: α=arctan(gR/v²). At 3m: tilt 15.8-27.5°. Nozzle cone: 7-10° half-angle (refined from 12°). Recoil ~1N (1-2% of thrust).
**Action:** Implement drop compensation. Update nozzle 3D STL. Accept 2-4m range.

### F-2026-06-03-060 [CRITICAL] — Legal/Regulatory Risks
Transport Canada: SFOC required for advertised events (even sub-250g). Payload spraying prohibited under CAR 901.02. Campus Safe Zones = no operation. Possible criminal charges (assault with weapon). Police lockdowns at Canadian schools for Senior Assassins.
**Action:** Off-campus only. Event permission. Brightly colored drone. Documented consent from targets.

### F-2026-06-03-061 [MEDIUM] — Coreless Gear Pump Alternative
Micro coreless gear pumps (12-15g, 400-600 mL/min) lighter than S18 (18g) but weak self-priming (gravity-feed required). RC fuel pumps (15-22g, 500+ mL/min). S18 still best for v1.
**Action:** S18 for v1. Coreless gear for v2 weight reduction.
