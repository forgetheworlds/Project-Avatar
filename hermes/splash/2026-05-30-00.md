## 2. 🔴 CRITICAL FAILURE MODES — Lessons from Failed Builds

### 2.1 Failure Mode #1: Newtonian Recoil Torque (Uncontrolled Yaw)

**Problem:** Firing water creates equal/opposite thrust. With nozzle panned 45° left, recoil pushes drone nose right → yaw destabilization → crash or missed target.

**Three mitigation strategies from community:**
| Strategy | Effectiveness | Complexity | Splash Viability |
|----------|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Tune FC yaw PID** to be more aggressive | 🟡 Moderate | Low (parameter change) | ✅ Primary |
| **Program counter-yaw mix** (EdgeTX or ArduPilot script: pump active → counter-yaw proportional to throttle) | 🟢 High | Medium (scripting needed) | ✅ Recommended |
| **Dual-nozzle counter-fire** (fire equal blast backward simultaneously) | 🟢 Very High | High (mechanical redesign) | ❌ Too heavy |

**Splash Action:** Add RCx_OPTION counter-yaw mix in ArduPilot. Set SERVO7_FUNCTION=22 (sprayer) → create mixer rule that applies -20% yaw when pump active. Test in SITL before flight.

---

### 2.2 Failure Mode #2: Fluid Sloshing (The "Pendulum Effect")

**Problem:** Water shifts inside partially-empty tank during banking → CG shift → PID over-correction → oscillations → crash.

**Solutions verified by community:**
| Solution | Weight | Effectiveness |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Flexible medical-grade IV fluid bladder (collapses as water depletes) | ~5g | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Baffled 3D-printed tank (cross-lattice internal structure) | ~10-15g (extra material) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Spring-pressurized syringe** (zero ullage, no slosh) | ~8g | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **(Best — already chosen for Splash)** |

**Splash Confirmation:** The spring-pressurized syringe approach (chosen in Hour 6) is validated as the best anti-slosh solution by the wider community. No change needed.

---

### 2.3 Failure Mode #3: Servo Stripping & Backlash Under Load

**Problem:** Aerodynamic drag + water-filled nozzle weight strips plastic gear servos during fast maneuvers.

**Verified solutions:**
- **MG90S** (metal gear) mandatory over SG90 (plastic) — confirmed
- For larger payloads: worm-gear assembly (self-locking, prevents backdriving)
- Digital servos > analog (higher holding torque, faster response)

**Splash Confirmation:** MG90S chosen in Hour 6 is correct. Metal gears critical for water payload.

---

### 2.4 Failure Mode #4: Capillary Water Ingress & Short Circuits

**Problem:** High-pressure mist drifts back into frame during flight → ESC or FC short circuit.

**Solutions:**
- Silicone conformal coating on ALL PCBs (MG Chemicals 422C or similar)
- Marine grease on servo output shaft seals
- 3D-printed splash guards around electronics stack
- ESP32-CAM mounted with lens facing forward, PCB protected

**Splash Action:** Add conformal coating to BOM. Design splash guard for MicoAir H743 and ESP32.

---

### 2.5 Failure Mode #5: Wi-Fi Frame Dropping & Latency Spikes from Motor EMI

**Problem:** ESP32-CAM video stream suffers signal degradation from motor/ESC electromagnetic interference.

**Solutions:**
| Solution | Improvement | Weight Cost |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| External omnidirectional antenna on ESP32-CAM | 50-80% fewer dropouts | ~1g |
| Lower stream to QVGA (320×240) at 15 FPS | 60% bandwidth reduction | 0g (software) |
| Shield ESP32 in copper tape | 80% EMI reduction | ~2g |
| Use 5.8GHz WiFi instead of 2.4GHz | Less crowded, less EMI overlap | Requires different module |

**Splash Action:** Plan for QVGA 320×240 at 15-30 FPS as fallback if 640×480 dropping frames. Add external antenna to ESP32-S3 CAM module.

### 3.3 New: Motion Compensation Architecture (IMU Gyro Telemetry)

Prior Hour 2 research covered motion compensation conceptually. Google AI Mode provided the **implementation architecture:**

```
[FPV Camera Feed] → [YOLO Inference] → [Motion Compensation] ← [IMU Gyro Telemetry]
                                              ↓
                                   [Kalman Filter State Estimator]
                                              ↓
                                   [PID + Feedforward Servo Controller]
                                              ↓
                                   [Pan/Tilt Servos via MAVLink]
```

**Key insight:** Drone ego-motion (pitch, roll, yaw) must be subtracted from target pixel coordinates using a homography transform:

```
R = Rodrigues(gyro_deltas)  # Rotation matrix from IMU
H = K @ R @ K_inv            # Homography matrix (K = camera intrinsics)
stabilized_point = H @ pixel_point  # Decoupled from drone motion
```

**Splash Impact:** This is CRITICAL for airborne operation. Without it, the turret will oscillate wildly as the drone pitches during flight. Add this to the CV pipeline implementation spec.
