Three of the five projects found were updated within the last 2 weeks, and CF-Drone was updated *today*. The ESP32 drone space is now seeing:
- **Professional-grade FCs** (cortex with DShot, LiDAR, GPS, blackbox) — competing with Betaflight on features
- **Web-native control** (CF-Drone, cifertech) — browser-based ground stations are becoming the norm
- **Academic rigor** (ElektroJonas) — universities are teaching drone control on ESP32
- **Ultra-low-cost** (AceMicroFlyer) — BOM under $30 for a flying drone

## Pattern: Dual-Core FreeRTOS is the Architecture Standard
Both cortex and the earlier esp32-flight-controller use dual-core designs: flight loop on one core, everything else (radio, telemetry, sensors, logging) on the other. This is the proven pattern for ESP32 drone firmware.

## Pattern: WebSockets + WiFi = Standard Ground Control
CF-Drone (20Hz), cifertech (20Hz telemetry), and ESP-FLY all use WiFi + WebSocket for ground control. The 20Hz rate is the sweet spot for browser-based control. Dedicated radio (nRF24L01, ELRS) gives lower latency but WiFi is simpler and enables phone control without extra hardware.

## Gap Identified: No ESP32 Autonomous Waypoint Navigation Project
Despite all these flight controllers, **none of them implement GPS waypoint navigation.** They handle stabilization, altitude hold, and position hold — but autonomous "go to waypoint A, then B, then C" is missing from the ESP32 ecosystem. This is Avatar's opportunity to be first.
