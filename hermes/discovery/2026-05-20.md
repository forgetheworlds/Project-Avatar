This saves weight and cost while matching or exceeding DJI stabilization quality.

## New ESP32 Variants
- **ESP32-P4**: Released Q1 2025. Dual-core 400MHz RISC-V, MIPI CSI (camera), MIPI DSI (display), hardware JPEG encoder, 32MB PSRAM support. Built for AI/CV at the edge. No drone projects using it yet — THIS IS THE OPPORTUNITY.
- **ESP32-S3**: Mature, dual-core Xtensa LX7, 240MHz, Wi-Fi 4 + BLE 5, vector extensions for ML. Used in ESP-FLY and MICRO-DRONE. Proven in drone builds.
- **ESP32-C6**: RISC-V, Wi-Fi 6 + BLE 5 + 802.15.4 (Thread/Zigbee). Interesting for mesh drone swarms but less compute than S3.

Key Lesson: **ESP32-S3 is the safe choice NOW.** ESP32-P4 is the aspirational choice for onboard AI/CV (cinematic subject tracking, obstacle avoidance). The P4's MIPI CSI interface means direct camera connection without the ESP32-CAM's JPEG compression bottleneck.

## Battery Tech for Micro Drones
- 1S LiHV (4.35V): 300-750mAh, 8-20g, used in tiny whoops
- 2S LiPo: 300-450mAh, 25-35g, for more power
- 18650 Li-Ion: 3000-3500mAh, 45-50g, best Wh/kg for endurance
- GaN (Gallium Nitride) chargers: faster charging, smaller, but not a battery change

Key Lesson: For Avatar's goals (cinematic, autonomous, GPS), **flight time is the priority over raw power.** A 2S 450mAh LiPo (~30g) with efficient coreless/brushless motors could achieve 8-12 minutes. Going 18650 Li-Ion could push to 15-20 minutes but adds weight.

# 6. Architecture Decision Analysis

## Option A: Pure ESP32-S3 FC (esp-drone based)
- Pros: Single MCU, lower cost, lower weight, full control over code
- Cons: Must implement GPS fusion, position hold, waypoint navigation from scratch; stability likely worse than Betaflight

## Option B: Betaflight FC + ESP32-S3 Sidecar
- Pros: Betaflight handles flight stability + GPS position hold; ESP32 handles cinematic logic + camera trigger + telemetry; proven FC stability
- Cons: Two MCUs (weight + complexity), MSP/CRSF communication overhead, power draw

**Recommendation: Option B for Phase 1.** Use a lightweight Betaflight AIO FC (e.g., Happymodel X12, ~3.5g) + ESP32-S3 sidecar. The Betaflight FC ensures stable flight from day one, while the ESP32 focuses on what it does best: WiFi connectivity, camera control, trajectory generation, and telemetry. Transition to pure ESP32 (Option A) only if weight/power become critical.
